Latest News 📢 (November 2026)

Our new preprint is online on arXiv. We propose the M3PI-DeepONet, a Modified Multi-Input Multi-Output Physics-Informed DeepONet for predicting unsteady 3D flows in an idealized Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm (AAA). The model predicts velocity and pressure fields with average relative L2 errors below 4% and around 5% respectively, with a conservative retained-cycle inference speed-up of about 36× over the reference CFD simulations.

O.L. Cruz-González, V. Deplano, B. Ghattas. Learning Unsteady Aneurysm Hemodynamics with Physics-Informed DeepONets. arXiv:2608.13629 [stat.ML]. doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.13629
